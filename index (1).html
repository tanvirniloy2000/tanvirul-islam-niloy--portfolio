About me

My B.Sc. in CSE (University of Science and Technology Chittagong, 2025) gave me the models: OSI and TCP/IP layering, addressing and protocol state machines. In ISP support I test them on live subscriber networks, where I work with MikroTik routers, PPPoE sessions, GPON ONUs, OLTs and VLANs.

I also have hands-on exposure to industrial automation: Delta and Siemens LOGO PLCs, VFDs and sensors. That background shapes how I troubleshoot, from the physical layer upward, changing one variable at a time and documenting the result. I’m completing CCNA and want to move into network engineering, security, cloud networking and automation.
